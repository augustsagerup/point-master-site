Questions people ask first

Do I need to give you my logins?

Not to get started. I can find and hold the seat, and you complete the transfer and booking yourself with step-by-step instructions. If you'd rather I handle it, logins are shared through a password manager, never by text or email, and covered by a written agreement. I never ask for card or passport numbers in a message.

Whose points do you use?

Yours, only. I don't buy, sell or broker points. Everything is booked on your own card and airline accounts, in your name.

Will you transfer my points without asking?

Never. Transfers between programs can't be reversed, so nothing moves until you've seen the exact seat, the points, the fees and said yes.

Which points work?

Amex Membership Rewards, Chase Ultimate Rewards, Citi ThankYou, Capital One, Bilt, and most major airline and hotel programs. Send me your balances and I'll tell you what they can reach.

What if my plans change?

I'll explain the change and cancellation rules for each option before you book. If something moves, I handle the rebooking.

Can't my card's concierge do this?

Card concierges are good at booking cash fares. Finding and catching partner award space on points is a different job, and it's the only one I do.

Where are you headed?

Send me the trip and roughly what points you have. I'll come back with what's realistic, usually the same day.
